# THE LEDGER — Engagement Beat ↔ Visual Reinforcement Map

Companion to `STYLE_GUIDE.md` and `SHOT_BOARDS.md`. The script's §3 timeline names 31
engagement beats across 24 minutes. Long-form retention research (the same dataset
used to inform the script's pacing) flags 7 *dip windows* where viewer attention
historically falls off if no delivery moment arrives. This document maps each beat to
the visual gesture that reinforces it — so the animator on `t_e781bf96` knows
*precisely* which seconds of finished video must contain a delivery.

The audit is one-directional: every dip window MUST contain a labeled delivery; every
*payoff* MUST be a visual moment, not a narration-only moment. Beats not on this map
are filler-recovery (they may have visuals, but they are not the engagement contract).

| 5.1 Push-in (Ken Burns accusatory)             | 6          | 1, 8, 9, 10, 13, 17        | low (varied subjects) |

| 5.3 Hard cut to black + name plate             | 11         | between every scene        | high — but it's the connective tissue, must be uniform |
| 5.4 Number theatre count-up                    | 4          | 1 (ladder), 2, 12 (stop-motion variant), 14, 15 | medium — variant types prevent staleness |
| 5.5 Ledger plate write-in                      | 1          | 11                         | none — single canonical use |
| 5.6 Held silence on a typography plate         | 4          | 13, 15, 16, 17, 18         | medium — used at decreasing intensity to avoid numbing |
| 7.1 Newspaper plate (environment)              | 6          | 1 strap, 2, 10, 13, 17, 18 | low (varied newspaper sources / scales) |
| 7.2 Ledger / balance sheet (environment)       | 4          | 1, 2, 11, 19               | low (each at increasing scale) |
| 7.3 Map (environment)                          | 2          | 7, 14                      | none — deliberate two-uses-only |

**Verdict:** distribution is healthy. The mechanism reveal (5.5) being a *singleton*
preserves its impact — it lands at 16:00 and never returns. The map (7.3) appears
twice and the second appearance (Bryan's rail tour) deliberately echoes the first
(Cleveland chokepoint) — same gesture, different scale, structural rhyme.

The held-silence gesture (5.6) appears four times in the back half (13, 15, 16, 17,
18), which is intentional: the documentary's tonal trajectory is from *number-theatre
density* in the front half to *typographic stillness* in the back half. The
distribution mirrors the script's pacing pivot.

## 5. Music + visual sync points

From `FULL_SCRIPT.md` §4, the music bed lifts ~3 dB at four moments. The visual must
land *exactly on the music lift*, not 0.5 s before or after:

| Time   | Music lift | Visual landing                                                  | Sync                |
|--------|------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------|---------------------|
| 2:30   | +3 dB      | `$3,500,000` count-up *freezes* on the lift                     | freeze on downbeat  |
| 16:00  | +3 dB      | Ledger plate write-in *first line* lands on the lift             | first line on lift  |
| 20:15  | +3 dB      | Verdict line `the ledger won` lands on the lift                  | text-in on lift     |
| 23:30  | +3 dB      | Vertical timeline *first row* (1896) stamps on the lift          | first row on lift   |

These are the four moments where the editor MUST conform visual and audio to the same
frame. Per LEARNINGS line 25 (punctuation does not control Kokoro pacing), narration
timing is locked at audio-render time and the music lift cues should be placed
against the rendered narration WAV, then the visual GSAP positions adjusted to match.
Order of operations:

1. Render per-scene narration (Voicebox George + atempo 1.18).
2. Place music bed against the rendered narration; mark lift cues at the four
   moments above.
3. Tune visual GSAP positions to land on the lift cues.

## 7. Failure modes (visual delivery audit)

Things to check on the assembly task before sign-off, in order:

1. **Every dip has a delivery.** Re-watch at 0:30, 3:00, 5:00, 10:00, 15:00, 19:00,
   21:30 — at each, the next 60 s must contain a *new* visual gesture (count-up,
   chapter card, name reveal, ledger, etc.). If any of these 60-s windows contains
   only Ken Burns drift on a held image, the dip is unsupported.
2. **Every hero text plate is on screen ≥ 1.0 s.** No sub-second appearances. The
   shorts project's audit (12 violations at sub-1.0s) is the regression baseline.
3. **No hero text below y≈880.** Verdict lines anchor at 200 px from bottom (= y≈880).
   Any text inside the y=880-y=1080 band collides with the lower thirds of the
   composition; LEARNINGS line 24 is the fix.
4. **Period filter on every photograph.** Sepia 0.25 / contrast 1.05 / brightness
   0.92 / saturation 0.85. Any raw archival JPEG that lands without the filter
   breaks the period unit.
5. **Music lift sync within 200 ms** of the four sync points. The visual editor
   should re-render any single frame mismatch at the four lift moments.
6. **Held-silence beats are not interrupted by music or motion.** The five silence
   moments must show: zero visual element changing in scale, position, or opacity;
   music bed at its base level (no lift); no narration. If the music bed has a
   rest, it stays at rest through the silence.
