### 3. `mckinley-front-porch-1896.jpg` — McKinley front-porch crowd photograph (Canton OH, October 1896)

- **Source page**: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Front_porch_campaign_2.jpg
- **Direct image (Wikimedia full-res, 11030 × 8794)**: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/8/86/Front_porch_campaign_2.jpg
- **Wikimedia ID**: `File:Front_porch_campaign_2.jpg`
- **LoC ID**: n/a — held by the McKinley Presidential Library & Museum,
  Canton OH (photo folder "1896 campaign #1"). Not in the LoC P&P catalog.
  loc.gov was returning Cloudflare bot challenges during this procurement
  run; the Wikimedia mirror is the cleanest PD source available.
- **Photographer**: Luke C. Dillon (1844–1904), former Army of the Potomac
  photographer.
- **PD justification**: Photograph taken October 1896. Photographer died 1904
  (>120 yrs ago, life+70 expired). Published in the U.S. before 1929 →
  PD-US-expired. Wikimedia tag `{{PD-US-expired}}`.
- **Resolution as downloaded**: 11030 × 8794, 40 MB. Resized to a 2400px-wide
  derivative (1.4 MB) for browser performance; original kept at
  `mckinley-front-porch-1896.original.jpg`.
- **Wired into**: `compositions/scene-12-front-porch-fs.html` as a fullscreen
  plate with slow Ken Burns push (1.00 → 1.04 over 38s) and a top-and-bottom
  darkening gradient so the JULY/AUGUST/SEPTEMBER/OCTOBER counter strip and
  the verdict line stay legible over the photograph.
- **Note on subject**: vision QA flagged this as a posed delegation photo
  (≈22 men in formal attire on the porch) rather than a "crowd" photograph
  per se — it shows one of the many delegations that visited Canton during
  the front-porch campaign, with McKinley himself center-front. This is the
  canonical surviving PD photograph of the front-porch campaign and matches
  the shot board's "held wide static plate of Canton" prescription.
